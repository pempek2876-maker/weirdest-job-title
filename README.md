Entry for the make.fun competition **Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge
Showdown** (https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922):
one job title of at most 60 characters — the weirdest one we could think of.

## The entry

> Senior Apologizer for Billionaires' Group Chats

## How it was chosen

`main.ts` holds 14 candidate job titles, each scored on the judge's
published criteria in the judge's order: a weird job title first, then
stranger than ordinary work with a gloriously odd twist, then funny with a
twist that lands on the first read, then sounding like a real job specific
enough to picture the person doing it, then reading instantly. Every
candidate meets every other in a full round-robin (91 pairings); the winning
title took all 13 of its pairings — a real-sounding corporate title
("Senior X for Y") whose one twist is apologizing inside billionaires'
group chats. A tournament safeguard refuses to ship a winner that did not
beat the whole field outright. The program is deterministic (two runs,
byte-identical output) and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it
locally with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
